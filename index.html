Our Approach

We Train Students Till the Very End

Most workshops stop at a certificate. We stay with students until their idea becomes a working prototype, a pilot deployment and a research paper.

1

Idea & Problem Definition

Identify real-world problems, finalize a feasible idea, and plan the architecture, components and timeline.

2

Prototype Development

Hands-on hardware and software build with ESP32, Raspberry Pi, ROS, AI models and more, with mentor guidance at every step.

3

Pilot Deployment

Test the prototype in a real environment such as a campus, lab, farm or small industry, and refine it using real data.

4

Research Paper Writing

Structuring results, literature review, formatting and publication guidance for conferences and journals.
